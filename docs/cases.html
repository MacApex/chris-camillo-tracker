怎么读这张图，以及我们不做什么

How to read the chart, and what we will not do

曲线是 2026 年 11 月 13 日取到的真实历史序列，带来源。取不到的序列标为缺失，绝不插值、填零或编造。Yahoo 评级为空是证据不足，不是「卖方沉默」，也不记成零日时差。成交日未知时，不发布单一数字的交易时差，也不用月中占位冒充成交。短期社会或搜索观察不是利润、现金流或价值。这些案例是故事讲出来之后挑选的，有后见、幸存和选择偏差。新冠仓位含看跌期权和直接做空，股价路径不是损益。Tapestry 的日期是 2021 年 8 月 18 日的发表日，不是成交日。Restaurant Brands 的论点是 Whopper 与 Popeyes，不是 Tim Hortons 翻盘。Lululemon 在该帖里只是搜索可靠性的例子，帖子没有建立持仓或退出。

时差只在同时满足以下条件时计算：有记录的进场（日或月精度，后者会标明），以及之后 Yahoo 样本里真实、有日期的评级上调或财报超预期。同一评级下几美元的目标微调、进场之前卖方已经偏多，都不构成「他领先了」。Google Trends 使用该案例的自定义时间窗（不是默认的「最近五年」）；数值是该窗内的相对 0–100。

Every line is a real historical series retrieved on 13 Nov 2026, with provenance. A missing series stays missing — never interpolated, zero-filled, or invented. An empty Yahoo rating history is insufficient evidence, not a silent Street, and not a zero-day lead. Where the execution day is unknown, there is no single-number trading lead and no mid-month placeholder pretending to be a fill. A short-term social or search observation is not earnings, cash flow, or value. These cases were chosen after the stories were told: look-ahead, survivorship, and selection bias apply. The COVID positions included puts and outright shorts; the equity path is not that P&L. The Tapestry date is the 18 Aug 2021 publication date, not a fill. The Restaurant Brands thesis was the Whopper and the Popeyes sandwich, not a Tim Hortons turnaround. Lululemon in that thread is a search-reliability example; the thread does not establish a position or an exit.

Lead time is computed only when there is a documented entry (day or month precision, the latter labeled) and a real, dated rating upgrade or earnings beat in the Yahoo sample after it. A few-dollar target trim on an unchanged rating, or a Street that was already constructive before the entry, does not count as a lead. Google Trends uses an explicit custom timeframe for that case (not the default “past five years”); values are relative 0–100 inside that window.

数据从哪里来，以及仍然缺什么

Where the data comes from, and what is still missing

  • 股价、评级、财报。 Yahoo Finance，经 yfinance 1.8，2026 年 11 月 13 日（Asia/Shanghai）拉取。复权收盘。评级索引先排序再按日期过滤（该索引不是单调的，不能直接切片）。Yahoo 历史不完整。
  • Google Trends。 pytrends，美国，每个案例一段明确的自定义时间窗。这和默认的「最近五年」不是同一件事：自定义窗口取到了更早的相对指数，包括库存里 ELF「elf cosmetics」在周标签 2019-03-03 为 100。周标签的起点不是序列变得可获得的时间，整窗里的峰值是事后选出的，有前视。本次没有重拉这条序列。数值是窗内相对兴趣，不是查询次数。2025 年的帖子假设外部人工智能查询可能漏进 Trends，并用 Lululemon 产品词作例子；那是假设，不是已核实的持仓退出。
  • 维基浏览。 Wikimedia REST，英文维基，2015 年起有数据。e.l.f. 使用条目 E.l.f._Cosmetics（原先错误的 E.l.f. 返回 404，已更正，没有编造）。ELF 条目本身流量很低，另附 Jeffree Star 同期浏览，并标明那不是品牌条目，也不是某一支视频的上传日（该日期没有独立来源）。新冠条目 COVID-19_pandemic 的浏览从 2020 年 3 月 12 日开始，更早是缺失，不是零；并且那不是他的中文一手来源。
  • 有日期的社会证据。 只使用来源里已经写明的日期：Dorel 2020 年 4 月 24 日的截图与 11 月退出（BI）、Crocs 2022 年 8 月 3 日仍在持有（Benzinga）、新冠自称 3 月初退出。Jeffree Star 视频没有独立核实的上传日，故不设标记。
  • 远期回报。 沿用已发表的事件研究（街景），不在本页另算一套未经核对的路径。

原始序列与出处：data/cases/，图数据：docs/data/cases-charts.json。配套说明见 research/09 与 方法论总纲。

  • Prices, ratings, earnings. Yahoo Finance via yfinance 1.8, retrieved 13 Nov 2026 (Asia/Shanghai). Auto-adjusted closes. Ratings are sorted, then filtered by date (the index is not monotonic and cannot be sliced directly). Yahoo’s history is incomplete.
  • Google Trends. pytrends, US, with an explicit custom timeframe for each case. That is not the same query as the default five-year view: custom windows did return earlier relative-interest series, including a stored “elf cosmetics” value of 100 on the week label 2019-03-03. A week-label start is not the time the series became available, and a peak picked inside the full window is retrospective. This pass did not re-pull that series. Values are relative interest inside the window, not query counts. A 2025 thread hypothesizes that external AI queries can leak into Trends and uses Lululemon product terms as an example. That is a hypothesis, not a verified position or exit.
  • Wikipedia pageviews. Wikimedia REST, English Wikipedia, available from 2015. e.l.f. uses the article E.l.f._Cosmetics (the earlier title E.l.f. returned 404; it was corrected, not invented). The ELF article itself is low-traffic; Jeffree Star pageviews are attached as a contemporaneous series, labeled as not the brand article and not a dated video upload (that date was not independently sourced). Pageviews for COVID-19_pandemic begin on 12 Mar 2020; earlier days are missing, not zero; and that article is not his Chinese-language primary sources.
  • Dated social evidence. Only dates already in the sources: Dorel’s 24 Apr 2020 screenshots and November exit (BI), Crocs still long on 3 Aug 2022 (Benzinga), a self-reported early-March COVID exit. No Jeffree Star video-upload marker is drawn, because that date was not independently verified.
  • Forward returns. The published event study on Street view, not a new unaudited path computed on this page.
