方法精髓

Essence

五句话讲方法

Five lines on the method

  1. 观察这个世界——商店、评论、Trends、朋友——直到出现一次有意义的变化，而不是每一个潮流。
  2. 把它连到一家上市公司：变化必须大到能推动业绩，并确认卖方尚未定价。
  3. 优势是时差（信息不平衡），不是更便宜的估值。进得早；在事实进入财报、研报和价格本身时退出。
  4. 他忽略图表和传统基本面，不用止损，按信念决定仓位（他曾说 2%–30%），并经常在一个单独的「大钱」账户里用期权表达观点。
  5. 这种杠杆和集中，是审计业绩的承重墙——也是在没有同样过程和缓冲时，最危险的模仿对象。
  1. Watch the world — stores, comments, Trends, friends — until a meaningful change appears, not every fad.
  2. Connect it to a public company where the change can actually move the needle, and confirm the Street has not already priced it.
  3. The edge is a time gap (information imbalance), not a cheaper multiple. Enter early; exit as the fact reaches earnings, notes, and the tape.
  4. He ignores charts and traditional fundamentals, uses no stop-losses, sizes by conviction (he has said 2–30%), and often expresses the view with options inside a separate “big money” account.
  5. That leverage and concentration are load-bearing for the audited returns — and the most dangerous things to copy without the same process or cushion.
