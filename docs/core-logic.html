核心投资逻辑

Core logic and a reusable playbook

先看世界，再看华尔街。

Look at the world first, then at Wall Street.

第一性原理

社会套利是信息差投资。步骤只有四句：观察真实世界中已经发生的变化（文化、消费、技术、政策）；把它连接到一家足够大、该变化能够影响利润的上市公司；在华尔街把这件事写入盈利预测和分析师报告之前建立仓位；当信息进入财报、研报和价格本身、信息差消失时退出。

Schwager 给这一章的标题是 “Neither”——既不是传统基本面，也不是技术分析。彼得·林奇是灵感来源（走进商场、观察生活），但 Camillo 主动放弃了林奇式的财务深挖，只保留规模与物质性。边缘来自时差，不是来自同一个模型上的更聪明。

后来的工具（TikTok、Google Trends、TickerTags、Discord、专业论坛）都只是观察装置。2025 年他退出 Lululemon 的公开理由是：AI 污染了 Trends，工具本身坏了。方法的核心不是某一个 App。

First principles

Social arbitrage is information-gap investing. Observe a real-world change (culture, consumption, technology, policy). Connect it to a public company large enough for the change to matter. Invest before Wall Street writes it into estimates and notes. Exit as the fact reaches earnings, research, and the tape — at information parity.

Schwager titled the chapter “Neither”: neither traditional fundamental analysis nor technical analysis. Peter Lynch is the stated inspiration (walk the mall), but Camillo drops Lynch’s financial deep-dive and keeps size and materiality. The edge is being early, not being clever on the same model.

TikTok, Google Trends, TickerTags, Discord, and specialist forums are instruments of observation. In 2025 he said he exited Lululemon because AI had corrupted Trends — the tool itself had broken. The method is not an app.

决策树

Decision tree

信号评分标准

  • 基线，不是绝对值。 问的是「相对这个社群、这个季节、这个品牌平时的样子，是否异常」，而不是「今天的讨论是否很多」。
  • 交叉验证。 两个或三个独立平台同时上升，权重大于单一尖峰。本工具箱用各平台相对自身历史的 z 分数，并记录缺失，不填空。
  • 来源可靠性。 TikTok / 真实世界观察在 Camillo 框架里权更高；主流新闻量往往是滞后指标；Wikipedia 浏览量是注意力，不是情绪。
  • 查询质量。 短代码（如 ELF）在 X 上被去掉 $ 后会严重噪声。不能核实的数字一律丢弃。
  • 工具会坏。 2025 年 Trends 的 AI 污染是一个必须写进清单的失效模式。

Signal rubric

  • Baseline, not a raw count. The question is whether something is unusual versus that community, season, and brand — not whether “there is a lot of talk today.”
  • Corroboration. A rise on two or three independent platforms outweighs a single spike. This toolkit uses z-scores versus each platform’s own history, records missingness, and never imputes a count.
  • Source reliability. TikTok and lived observation sit higher in Camillo’s own practice; mainstream news volume often lags; Wikipedia pageviews are attention, not sentiment.
  • Query quality. Short tokens (ELF) become noisy once X strips the $ character. Unverifiable numbers are discarded.
  • Tools break. The 2025 AI-pollution of Google Trends is a first-class failure mode.

物质性检验

Materiality

  1. 这个变化触及的是公司的哪一条产品线？大约占总收入或利润的多少？
  2. 1–2% 的收入贡献通常不够（Elmer’s slime 是他用杠杆去打的边界案例，不是普通投资者的模板）。
  3. 公司是否够大、够流动性，能承受你计划的工具？他又说原则上不做微型股。
  4. 如果无法粗估物质性，就还没有到「候选」这一步。
  1. Which line of the company does this change touch, and roughly what share of sales or profit?
  2. A 1–2% revenue contribution is usually too small (Elmer’s slime is a leveraged edge-case he described, not a retail template).
  3. Is the name large and liquid enough for the instrument you have in mind? He has also said he does not, as a rule, invest in microcaps.
  4. If you cannot sketch materiality, it is not yet a candidate.

定价检验：已经定价了吗？

Is it priced in?

  1. 共识评级分布：是否已经一边倒买入？
  2. 目标价离散与隐含上行：卖方是否已经写入看多情形？
  3. 盈利预测是否在近 30 天内上修？
  4. 近 90 天是否出现集中上调？
  5. 研报 / 新闻语气、覆盖人数、历史超预期。
  6. 结论只能是：已定价 / 部分定价 / 尚未清楚定价 / 数据不足。后一项必须说出来，不能假装知道。
  1. Consensus rating distribution: is the Street already mostly a buy?
  2. Price-target dispersion and implied upside: is a bull case already in the numbers?
  3. Have EPS estimates been revised up over ~30 days?
  4. A cluster of upgrades in 90 days is a migration signal.
  5. Note/news tone, coverage count, surprise history.
  6. The only allowed labels are already / partially / not clearly priced / insufficient data. The last one must be said out loud.

退出清单

Exit checklist

  1. 信息是否已经进入最近一期财报或电话会？
  2. 分析师是否开始撰写你当初看到的那个事实？
  3. 他描述的退出是每次卖掉约 10–20%，直到信息平价；不是技术止损。
  4. 论点破坏（事实被证伪、工具被污染、你再也说不清物质性）是退出理由——即便他本人不用止损单。
  5. 不要因为「别人也这么看」而提前砍掉仍然成立的论点（这是他最后悔的一笔，代码未公开）。
  1. Has the fact entered the latest earnings or call?
  2. Have analysts started writing the thing you originally observed?
  3. He has described selling 10–20% at a time until information parity — not a chart stop.
  4. A broken thesis (the fact is false, the tool is polluted, materiality no longer holds) is an exit, even though he himself does not use stop-loss orders.
  5. Do not liquidate a still-valid thesis because other people are talking (his most-regretful trade, ticker unnamed).

普通投资者的风险规则

Risk rules for a normal investor

  1. 不要复制他所说的 2–30% 仓位，更不要复制「储蓄的 25–30% 去做 SPY / Wynn 看跌期权」。
  2. 默认用很小的仓位（例如 1–3%）做研究性跟踪；把期权溢价当作可以归零的金额。
  3. 他把「大钱」账户和安全账户分开；大多数人没有这种结构，也不应该用生活费去模拟。
  4. 他不用止损，可能在论点仍在时加仓——这对职业交易员是一回事，对用工资账户的人是另一回事。写一个「论点失效」退出条件。
  5. 一年可能只有一两次够格的想法（他 2011 年说 100–200 次观察换 1–2 笔交易）。没事可做是正常状态。
  6. 审计过的生涯回报不能当作你的期望收益。单笔盈亏多为自我陈述。
  1. Do not copy the 2–30% sizing he has described, and do not copy “25–30% of savings in SPY / Wynn puts.”
  2. Default to a small (e.g. 1–3%) research-sized line; treat any options premium as money you can lose in full.
  3. He separates a “big money” sleeve from a safer account. Most people do not have that structure and should not simulate it with living expenses.
  4. He uses no stops and may add when the thesis is intact. That is one thing for a professional book and another for a paycheck account. Write a thesis-break exit.
  5. One or two qualified ideas a year is consistent with what he said in 2011 (100–200 observations → 1–2 trades). Doing nothing is the default.
  6. The audited career record is not your expected return. Single-trade P&Ls are mostly self-reported.

01 · DIIBF · 2020-04-24 · 记者看过买单截图

01 · DIIBF · 24 Apr 2020 · purchase screenshots viewed

Dorel：日期最清楚的一笔

Dorel: the best-dated trade

信号是疫情下的居家与自行车需求。Business Insider 看过 2020 年 4 月 24 日、价格 1.56 美元的买入截图；约在 6 月于 4.46 美元附近分批减仓，11 月出清当天股价 11.38 美元。629% 是这段股价路径，不是已核实的个人收益率（他是分批卖出的）。Stansberry 上「9 倍，我记得」是自我陈述。

Yahoo 在 ±90 天窗口内没有抓到评级变动（覆盖可能很薄，零不代表没有活动）。Wikipedia「Dorel Industries」在日后 14 天日均浏览 298，相对日前 243，只是轻微上升。从 4 月 24 日起，yfinance 复权价的前瞻收益约为 1 个月 +131%、3 个月 +243%、6 个月 +609%。注意：yfinance 起始价约 0.69 美元，与 BI 截图的 1.56 美元不一致——场外报价质量有问题，两套数字我们都保留，不私下调和。

The signal was stay-at-home and bike demand. BI viewed purchase screenshots at $1.56 on 24 April 2020; gradual selling around $4.46 in June; a full exit in November when the stock was $11.38. The 629% is that stock path, not a verified personal rate of return (he sold gradually). “9X I think” on Stansberry is self-reported.

Yahoo’s upgrades/downgrades window is empty (thin coverage; a zero is not proof of inactivity). Wikipedia attention rose only modestly (243 → 298 daily average, 14d before vs after). Forward yfinance returns from 24 April: about +131% / +243% / +609% at 1/3/6 months. Caveat: yfinance’s starting close is about $0.69, which does not match the $1.56 screenshot. OTC data quality is the likely issue; we keep both figures and do not silently reconcile them.

02 · ELF · 2019-03（月精度）· 自我陈述 + 可核市场路径

02 · ELF · March 2019 (month precision) · self-reported + a checkable market path

e.l.f. 与 Jeffree Star：卖方随后跟上

e.l.f. and Jeffree Star: the Street followed

他对 BI 说，Jeffree Star 与渠道核实指向需求，股价在六周内上涨超过 50%。我们用 3 月 15 日作为月中占位（不是他的精确入场）。由此计算的市场路径是 1 个月 +25%、3 个月 +32.5%、6 个月 +70.3%——与「六周 50%」并不自动吻合，可能入场更早，我们不强制对齐。

有意思的是卖方：±90 天里，日期之前 0 次上调、之后 4 次上调。这是「信息向华尔街迁移」的一个可观察痕迹，也是定价检验要抓的东西。2019 年该 Wikipedia 条目不可用（404），注意力序列缺席。

He told BI that Jeffree Star and channel checks pointed to demand, and that the stock rose more than 50% in six weeks. We use 15 March as a mid-month placeholder, not his exact entry. The market path from that date is +25% / +32.5% / +70.3% at 1/3/6 months — which does not automatically equal “50% in six weeks.” The entry may have been earlier. We do not force a reconciliation.

The Street trace is the interesting part: in a ±90-day Yahoo window, 0 upgrades before the date and 4 after. That is a visible migration of information, which is what the priced-in test is for. Wikipedia has no 2019 series for the current “E.l.f.” title (404).

03 · QSR · 2019-04 与 2019-08 · 记者看过截图 · 亏损

03 · QSR · April and August 2019 · screenshots viewed · a loss

Restaurant Brands：方法不会让你永不犯错

Restaurant Brands: the method will not keep you from being wrong

Tim Hortons 翻转论点没有按预期走。BI 看过买入截图；他自称约亏损 75 万美元。股价后来从高点约 78.45 美元到 2020 年 3 月约跌 59%——这是市场路径，不是精确个人盈亏。我们事件研究里，4 月 15 日起 1/3/6 个月是 +0.5% / +9.2% / +7.1%（前段并不立刻为负）；8 月 15 日第二次买入窗口是 −2.2% / −7.2% / −7.3%。亏损出现在更长的持有期和 2020 年崩盘里。

这一案例必须留在库里，避免「只收集赢家」。观察方法不保证正确的公司，也不保证正确的时间。

A Tim Hortons turnaround thesis did not work. BI viewed purchase screenshots; he self-reported a loss of about $750k. The stock later fell about 59% from a $78.45 peak into March 2020 — a market path, not his exact P&L. In our event study, from 15 April the 1/3/6-month path is +0.5% / +9.2% / +7.1% (not immediately negative); the August window is −2.2% / −7.2% / −7.3%. The damage sits in a longer hold and the 2020 crash.

This row has to stay in the database so the archive is not winners-only. Observational method does not guarantee the right company or the right time.

04 · SPY / WYNN 看跌 · 2020-02 · 自我陈述

04 · SPY / WYNN puts · February 2020 · self-reported

新冠空头：不是 TikTok，是外语一手材料

The COVID shorts: not TikTok, but foreign-language primary sources

他在 Stansberry 说，借助翻译阅读中文论文，用储蓄的 25–30% 买入 SPY 与 Wynn 的看跌期权；起初亏损，3 月初「一周七位数」。未审计，也没有公开的行权价。从 2 月 18 日（2 月 15 日之后的交易日）起，SPY 1 个月 −28.7%、3 个月 −11.9%、6 个月已转正 +1.4%；Wynn 为 −67.5% / −38.4% / −36.9%。这些是标的路径，不是期权盈亏。早期对空头有利，六个月后指数已收回。

对普通投资者：这个仓位和工具都不可模仿。它说明两件事——信号可以来自专业或外语来源，不只是消费 App；以及退出时点（信息平价）比「看对方向」更决定期权结果。

On Stansberry he said he read Chinese-language papers via Translate, put 25–30% of savings into SPY and Wynn puts, lost at first, then made “seven figures in a week” in early March. Unaudited; strikes unpublished. From 18 February (the first session on or after 15 February), SPY was −28.7% / −11.9% / +1.4% at 1/3/6 months; Wynn −67.5% / −38.4% / −36.9%. These are underlying paths, not put P&L. The short side was favored early; the index had recovered by six months.

For a normal investor this size and instrument are not a template. The lessons that do transfer: a signal can come from specialist or foreign-language sources, not only consumer apps; and the exit at information parity matters more for options than “getting the direction right.”

05 · LULU 退出 · 2025 · 自我陈述

05 · LULU exit · 2025 · self-reported

当工具坏了：AI 污染 Google Trends

When the tool breaks: AI pollutes Google Trends

他在 2025 年的 X 长文（Threadreader 1957441296798298509）中说，因为 AI 生成查询污染了 Trends，他退出了 Lululemon 论点。这是方法的元教训：单一平台的尖峰，在无法被其他信号确认时，不是入场理由，而是失效警报。本工具箱因此要求 2–3 个平台交叉验证，并把 Trends 的可靠性写成带警告的权重，而不是最高权威。

In a 2025 X thread (Threadreader 1957441296798298509) he said he exited a Lululemon thesis after concluding that AI-generated queries had corrupted Google Trends. The meta-lesson: a single-platform spike that other signals will not confirm is not an entry — it is a failure alarm. That is why this toolkit requires 2–3-platform corroboration and treats Trends as a weighted, caveat-bearing input, not as an oracle.

常见陷阱

  • 把自我陈述的盈亏或营销数字（米歇尔·奥巴马 J.Crew「186%」——他其实错过了）当成审计结果。
  • 单一平台尖峰，尤其是已被 AI 污染的 Trends。
  • 物质性不够仍用杠杆（slime 是他的边界，不是你的）。
  • 已经定价还硬做（我们把这标为 late_or_crowded）。
  • 个人偏好压过流程（他承认的 Silicon Image HDMI 失败）。
  • 听别人的意见砍掉仍成立的论点（最后悔的一笔）。
  • 把迷因挤兑（GME / AMC 的自我陈述参与）当成典型的信息差交易。
  • 把 Pabst、Vegas Golden Knights 这类无来源传闻写进样本。
  • 在没有卖方数据时假装已经做完定价检验。
  • 把本扫描器的分数当成交易信号。

模板

论点与复盘模板在仓库 research/templates/：先写观察、基线、物质性、定价检验、工具与退出，再动手；事后对照「哪个环节断了」。

Pitfalls

  • Treating self-reported P&L or marketing figures (the Michelle Obama J.Crew “186%” — he missed it) as audited results.
  • A single-platform spike, especially in AI-polluted Trends.
  • Leverage on something too small to matter (slime is his edge-case, not yours).
  • Acting after the Street has already priced it (we tag this late_or_crowded).
  • Personal bias over process (his Silicon Image HDMI failure).
  • Cutting a still-valid thesis because of other people’s opinions (his most-regretful trade).
  • Treating meme-squeeze participation (his self-reported GME / AMC) as a typical information-gap trade.
  • Admitting unsourced attributions (Pabst, Vegas Golden Knights) into the sample.
  • Faking a priced-in test when Street data is missing.
  • Treating a scanner score as a trade signal.

Templates
