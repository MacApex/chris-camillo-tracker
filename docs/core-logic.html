第一性原理

社会套利是信息差投资。步骤只有四句：观察真实世界中已经发生的变化（文化、消费、技术、政策）；把它连接到一家足够大、该变化能够影响利润的上市公司；在华尔街把这件事写入盈利预测和分析师报告之前建立仓位；当信息进入财报、研报和价格本身、信息差消失时退出。

Schwager 给这一章的标题是 “Neither”——既不是传统基本面，也不是技术分析。彼得·林奇是灵感来源（走进商场、观察生活），但 Camillo 主动放弃了林奇式的财务深挖，只保留规模与物质性。边缘来自时差，不是来自同一个模型上的更聪明。

后来的工具（TikTok、Google Trends、TickerTags、Discord、专业论坛）都只是观察装置。2025 年的一条帖子假设外部人工智能查询可能漏进 Trends，并用 Lululemon 产品词举例，同时披露做空 GOOGL。帖子没有建立 Lululemon 持仓或退出。来源不可靠意味着不确定和重新评估，不是自动判定公司论点为假。方法的核心不是某一个 App。

First principles

Social arbitrage is information-gap investing. Observe a real-world change (culture, consumption, technology, policy). Connect it to a public company large enough for the change to matter. Invest before Wall Street writes it into estimates and notes. Exit as the fact reaches earnings, research, and the tape — at information parity.

Schwager titled the chapter “Neither”: neither traditional fundamental analysis nor technical analysis. Peter Lynch is the stated inspiration (walk the mall), but Camillo drops Lynch’s financial deep-dive and keeps size and materiality. The edge is being early, not being clever on the same model.

TikTok, Google Trends, TickerTags, Discord, and specialist forums are instruments of observation. A 2025 thread hypothesizes that external AI queries can leak into Trends, uses Lululemon product terms as the example, and discloses a short in GOOGL. The thread does not establish a Lululemon position or exit. An unreliable source means uncertainty and a reassessment, not an automatic false business thesis. The method is not an app.

决策树

Decision tree

信号评分标准

  • 基线，不是绝对值。 问的是「相对这个社群、这个季节、这个品牌平时的样子，是否异常」，而不是「今天的讨论是否很多」。
  • 交叉验证。 两个或三个独立平台同时上升，权重大于单一尖峰。本工具箱用各平台相对自身历史的 z 分数，并记录缺失，不填空。
  • 来源可靠性。 TikTok / 真实世界观察在 Camillo 框架里权更高；主流新闻量往往是滞后指标；Wikipedia 浏览量是注意力，不是情绪。
  • 查询质量。 短代码（如 ELF）在 X 上被去掉 $ 后会严重噪声。不能核实的数字一律丢弃。
  • 工具会坏。 2025 年 Trends 的 AI 污染是一个必须写进清单的失效模式。

Signal rubric

  • Baseline, not a raw count. The question is whether something is unusual versus that community, season, and brand — not whether “there is a lot of talk today.”
  • Corroboration. A rise on two or three independent platforms outweighs a single spike. This toolkit uses z-scores versus each platform’s own history, records missingness, and never imputes a count.
  • Source reliability. TikTok and lived observation sit higher in Camillo’s own practice; mainstream news volume often lags; Wikipedia pageviews are attention, not sentiment.
  • Query quality. Short tokens (ELF) become noisy once X strips the $ character. Unverifiable numbers are discarded.
  • Tools break. The 2025 AI-pollution of Google Trends is a first-class failure mode.

物质性检验

Materiality

  1. 谁是付费客户，经过哪条渠道和哪个地区，卖的是消耗还是铺货？
  2. 件数 × 净价 → 发行人确认的收入 → 增量毛利 → 费用和其他部门的抵消 → 税和股数 → 每股收益或自由现金流。未知保持「待研究」。没有「1%–2% 通常不够」这条规则。
  3. 债务、稀释、供给、促销和其他分部要单独写。写不出就留空。
  4. 这是项目约定，不是他公布的物质性公式。
  1. Who pays, through which channel and geography, and is it sell-through or stocking?
  2. Units × net price → recognized revenue → incremental gross profit → opex and other divisions → tax and share count → EPS or free cash flow. Unknowns stay “needs research.” There is no rule that 1–2% of revenue is usually insufficient.
  3. Write debt, dilution, supply, promotions, and the other divisions. If you cannot, leave the cell empty.
  4. This is a project convention, not a materiality formula he published.

辅助核对：所审材料是否反映这条论点？

Auxiliary check: is this thesis in the rows reviewed?

  1. 只核对已经写下来的那条论点，不用共识给股票打分或筛选。
  2. 所审评级、修正、目标价里，这条论点是明确反映、部分反映、未见，还是证据不足？
  3. 空行不是「没看见」，也不是「未定价」。
  4. 分数是项目启发式，不是已验证模型，也不能变成买入。
  1. Check only the thesis already written. Do not score or screen the stock by consensus.
  2. In the ratings, revisions, and targets reviewed, is that thesis explicitly reflected, partly reflected, not evidenced, or insufficient?
  3. An empty row is not “unaware” and not “unpriced.”
  4. The score is a project heuristic, not a validated model, and not a buy.

退出清单

Exit checklist

  1. 信息平价只是一种退出想法。它不能代替流动性、偿债能力、估值、跳空、相关敞口或组合亏损上限。
  2. 价格下跌本身从不是加仓理由。来源不可靠意味着不确定和重新评估，不是自动判定生意论点为假。
  3. 若讨论期权：到期、隐含波动，以及权利金可以全部损失。教育上的默认是不用杠杆。
  4. 分开的账户不能消除家庭风险。本页不设默认研究仓位。
  5. 这些是项目约定，不是 Camillo 方法的证明。
  1. Information parity is only one exit idea. It does not replace liquidity, solvency, valuation, gap risk, correlated exposures, or a portfolio-loss limit.
  2. A lower price alone never warrants adding. An unreliable source means uncertainty and a reassessment, not an automatic false business thesis.
  3. If options are discussed at all: expiry, implied volatility, and total loss of premium. No leverage is the educational default.
  4. A separate account does not remove household risk. This page sets no default research size.
  5. These are project conventions, not proof of Camillo’s method.

普通投资者的风险规则

Risk rules for a normal investor

  1. 不要把他自述的 2%–30% 或储蓄的 25%–30% 做成规则。
  2. 不设默认研究仓位。仓位若要写，必须单独论证，并保持「待研究」直到写出来。
  3. 分开的账户不能消除家庭风险。不用杠杆是教育上的默认。
  4. 他曾说不用止损。那是报道，不是本项目的认可。价格下跌本身不是加仓理由。
  5. 记者报道 Schwager 审阅过月度账单、Adebambo 独立看过并同意。那不是对每一笔交易或后来头条总数的公开发表审计，也不能当作期望收益。
  1. Do not turn his reported 2–30%, or 25–30% of savings, into a rule.
  2. There is no default research size. A size, if written, has to be argued on its own and stays “needs research” until it is.
  3. A separate account does not remove household risk. No leverage is the educational default.
  4. He has said he uses no stops. That is a report, not an endorsement here. A lower price alone is not a reason to add.
  5. A journalist reported that Schwager reviewed monthly statements and that Adebambo agreed. That is not a published audit of every trade or of later headline totals, and it is not an expected return.

01 · DIIBF · 2020-04-24 · 记者看过买单截图

01 · DIIBF · 24 Apr 2020 · purchase screenshots viewed

Dorel：日期最清楚的一笔

Dorel: the best-dated trade

信号是疫情下的居家与自行车需求。Business Insider 看过 2020 年 4 月 24 日、价格 1.56 美元的买入截图；约在 6 月于 4.46 美元附近分批减仓，11 月出清当天股价 11.38 美元。629% 是这段股价路径，不是已核实的个人收益率（他是分批卖出的）。Stansberry 上「9 倍，我记得」是自我陈述。

1.56 美元是截图上的名义价格。库存里的自动复权收盘约 0.69 美元。在调整因子、货币、证券身份和报价质量对齐之前，两者不能比较，这里也不假设原因。复权序列上的远期百分比是另一条价格定义，不是 629%，也不是每股收益。窗口内没有 Yahoo 评级行：证据不足，不是「卖方没看见」。维基浏览不是需求。

The signal was stay-at-home and bike demand. BI viewed purchase screenshots at $1.56 on 24 April 2020; gradual selling around $4.46 in June; a full exit in November when the stock was $11.38. The 629% is that stock path, not a verified personal rate of return (he sold gradually). “9X I think” on Stansberry is self-reported.

$1.56 is the nominal figure on the screenshots. The stored auto-adjusted close is about $0.69. Until adjustment factor, currency, security identity, and quote quality are reconciled, the two are not comparable, and no cause is assumed. Forward percentages on the adjusted series are a different price definition. They are not the 629% and not EPS. An empty Yahoo rating window is insufficient evidence, not a silent Street. Wikipedia pageviews are not demand.

04 · SPY / WYNN 看跌 · 2020-02 · 自我陈述

04 · SPY / WYNN puts · February 2020 · self-reported

新冠空头：不是 TikTok，是外语一手材料

The COVID shorts: not TikTok, but foreign-language primary sources

Stansberry 第 192 期里，他说当时在翻译中文报纸和博客，不是学术论文，然后买入看跌期权并直接做空 SPY，也提到 Wynn 的看跌期权。起初亏损，三月初「一周七位数」。这是自述，没有核实为完整平仓，行权价也没有公布。任何股价路径都是标的本身，不是期权或做空损益。

对普通投资者：这个仓位和工具都不可模仿。它说明两件事——信号可以来自专业或外语来源，不只是消费 App；以及退出时点（信息平价）比「看对方向」更决定期权结果。

On Stansberry episode 192 he said he was translating Chinese newspapers and blogs, not academic papers, then bought puts and shorted SPY outright, and also named Wynn puts. He was losing at first, then made “seven figures in a week” around early March. That is self-reported and is not a verified complete exit. Strikes are unpublished. Any equity path is the underlying, not put or short P&L.

For a normal investor this size and instrument are not a template. The lessons that do transfer: a signal can come from specialist or foreign-language sources, not only consumer apps; and the exit at information parity matters more for options than “getting the direction right.”

Search readings that do not match other signals: AI leakage is a hypothesis

2025 年的帖子（Threadreader 1957441296798298509）用 Lululemon 产品词举例：Trends 冲高，而刷卡、网页、应用和到店只有温和改善。他假设外部人工智能查询漏进了 Trends，并披露做空 GOOGL。帖子没有建立 Lululemon 持仓或退出。单一平台的尖峰不是利润。本工具箱的「两个平台 z」是项目启发式，不是他公布的规则，也不是已验证模型。

The 2025 thread (Threadreader 1957441296798298509) uses Lululemon product terms as an example: Trends spiked while card, web, app, and store signals improved only modestly. He hypothesizes that external AI queries leak into Trends, and he discloses a short in GOOGL. The thread does not establish a Lululemon position or exit. A single-platform spike is not earnings. The toolkit’s two-platform z rule is a project heuristic, not a rule he published and not a validated model.

常见陷阱

  • 把自我陈述的盈亏或营销数字（米歇尔·奥巴马 J.Crew「186%」——他其实错过了）当成审计结果。
  • 单一平台尖峰，尤其是已被 AI 污染的 Trends。
  • 物质性不够仍用杠杆（slime 是他的边界，不是你的）。
  • 已经定价还硬做（我们把这标为 late_or_crowded）。
  • 个人偏好压过流程（他承认的 Silicon Image HDMI 失败）。
  • 听别人的意见砍掉仍成立的论点（最后悔的一笔）。
  • 把迷因挤兑（GME / AMC 的自我陈述参与）当成典型的信息差交易。
  • 把 Pabst、Vegas Golden Knights 这类无来源传闻写进样本。
  • 在没有卖方数据时假装已经做完定价检验。
  • 把本扫描器的分数当成交易信号。

模板

论点与复盘模板在仓库 research/templates/：先写观察、基线、物质性、定价检验、工具与退出，再动手；事后对照「哪个环节断了」。

Pitfalls

  • Treating self-reported P&L or marketing figures (the Michelle Obama J.Crew “186%” — he missed it) as audited results.
  • A single-platform spike, especially in AI-polluted Trends.
  • Leverage on something too small to matter (slime is his edge-case, not yours).
  • Acting after the Street has already priced it (we tag this late_or_crowded).
  • Personal bias over process (his Silicon Image HDMI failure).
  • Cutting a still-valid thesis because of other people’s opinions (his most-regretful trade).
  • Treating meme-squeeze participation (his self-reported GME / AMC) as a typical information-gap trade.
  • Admitting unsourced attributions (Pabst, Vegas Golden Knights) into the sample.
  • Faking a priced-in test when Street data is missing.
  • Treating a scanner score as a trade signal.
