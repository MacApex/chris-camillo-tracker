方法论总纲

Methodology synthesis

时差，不是更便宜的估值。

A time gap, not a cheaper multiple.

把本档案已经核实的方法，收成一页总纲：第一性原理、因果链、七条真正要紧的规则、方法何时成立与失败，以及普通投资者该如何改写。证据链到既有页面，这里不重复长文。本页不是投资建议，也与 Camillo、Dumb Money、TickerTags 均无关联。

Everything we have verified, distilled to one page: first principles, the causal chain, the seven rules that actually matter, when the method works and fails, and what a normal investor should change. Evidence lives on the existing pages; this is not a repeat. This is not financial advice, and it is not affiliated with Camillo, Dumb Money, or TickerTags.

因果链

The causal chain

  1. 在真实世界里，观察到相对于你自己的基线（这个社群、这个季节、这个品牌平时的样子）的异常变化——文化、消费、技术或政策。不是相对于一条头条。
  2. 把它连接到一家上市公司：该变化必须大到足以推动这家公司的收入或利润（物质性）。
  3. 用 2–3 个独立平台交叉确认：各平台相对自身历史在上升。一条序列不是论点。终身累计播放量或单一畅销名次不是动量。
  4. 问卖方是否已经把这件事写入预测、评级和研报。已定价，就放下。
  5. 只有「利基上升，且尚未清楚定价」才进入人工研究：写下论点与退出条件，再决定是否行动。
  6. 退出看的是信息平价——事实进入财报、研报和价格本身——以及论点是否已被证伪（包括「测量工具坏了」）。
  1. Observe a real-world change — culture, consumption, technology, or policy — versus your own baseline for that community, season, and brand. Not versus a headline.
  2. Connect it to a public company where the change is large enough to move revenue or earnings (materiality).
  3. Corroborate on two or three independent platforms: each rising versus its own history. One series is not a thesis. A lifetime view total or a single bestseller rank is not momentum.
  4. Ask whether the Street has already written the fact into estimates, ratings, and notes. If it is priced, stand down.
  5. Only “niche rising and not clearly priced” becomes human work: write the thesis and the exit, then decide whether to act.
  6. Exit at information parity — the fact reaches earnings, notes, and the tape — and if the thesis is broken, including “the measuring tool is polluted.”

任何一关为「否」：停止，或继续观察。展开的决策树、清单与案例见 核心逻辑；十步操作见 方法手册；卖方框架与事件研究见 街景。

A “no” at any gate is a stop, or a decision to keep watching. The full tree, checklists and cases are on Core logic; the ten operational steps are in the Playbook; the Street framework and event study are on Street view.

七条真正要紧的规则

The seven rules that matter

  1. 问的是异常，不是热闹。 没有基线，评论再多也只是噪音。Camillo 对 Crocs 与 UGG 的说法是：他知道「平常」是多少。
  2. 边缘是时差，不是同一个模型上更聪明。 Schwager 把这一章题为 “Neither”：既非传统基本面，也非技术分析。灵感来自林奇，但财务深挖被主动放下，只保留规模与物质性。
  3. 一条序列不是论点。 至少两个独立平台同时相对自身基线上升。缺失的平台要记下来，不许填空。2025 年他退出 Lululemon，公开理由是 Google Trends 被人工智能查询污染——工具本身会坏。
  4. 先问能否推动利润，再谈故事。 打开 10-K 的分部说明，而不是做一份估值模型。slime 热潮对 Elmer’s 可能足够，对 Newell 整体仍可能只是四舍五入——要算，不要感觉。
  5. 已定价就放下。 信息一旦「被普遍知道」，他就认为已经在价格里。退出是分批的、按信息到达的，不是按一张价格目标图。
  6. 不能核实的数字，丢掉。 终身播放量是存量，要动量必须自己做重复取样——本项目不设每日快照例行。宽泛标签（如 #coach、#slime）不可当作品牌计数。查询会被污染（X 去掉 $ 之后，ELF 会严重噪声）。
  7. 不要复制他的风险结构。 审计业绩建立在期权、集中仓位（他曾说一般 2%–30%）和一个可以承受损失的「大钱」账户上。波动大约是指数的五倍。无止损只在「交易的是信息、论点未破」时自洽；借走这句话而不借过程，是危险的。
  1. The question is “unusual,” not “busy.” Without a baseline, a lot of talk is just noise. On Crocs and UGGs he has said he knows what ordinary comment volume looks like.
  2. The edge is a time gap, not a smarter take on the same model. Schwager titled the chapter “Neither”: neither traditional fundamentals nor technical analysis. Lynch is the inspiration; the financial deep-dive is dropped; size and materiality remain.
  3. One series is not a thesis. At least two independent platforms should be rising versus their own baselines. Missing platforms are recorded, never imputed. In 2025 he exited Lululemon after concluding that AI queries had polluted Google Trends — tools break.
  4. Materiality before the romance. Open the 10-K segment note, not a valuation model. A slime craze can matter at Elmer’s and still be a rounding error at Newell as a whole — compute it; don’t feel it.
  5. If it is priced, stand down. Once the information is “universally known,” he treats it as in the price. Exits are gradual and information-based, not a chart target.
  6. Discard unverifiable numbers. Lifetime view totals are a stock; a flow needs repeated snapshots at a cadence you choose — this project does not run a daily routine. Generic tags (#coach, #slime) are not brand counts. Queries get polluted (after X strips the $ character, ELF is noisy).
  7. Do not copy his risk structure. The audited record sits on options, concentration (he has said generally 2–30%), and a “big money” account funded with money he can afford to lose. Volatility was about five times the index. “No stop-losses” is coherent only inside an information process; borrowing the phrase without the process is dangerous.

何时成立

When it works

  • 变化发生在卖方听得少的地方：青年与女性消费文化、地理口袋（他所说的「地理套利」）。
  • 观察者对该领域有真正的基线，而不是第一次看到热闹。
  • 变化对那家公司的利润够大，且尚未进入预测与研报。
  • 往往靠近一次披露：货架变化之后几天就是财报，这是他故事里反复出现的节奏，不是「买热门品牌拿十年」。
  • The change sits where the Street under-listens: youth and female consumer culture, geographic pockets (his “geographical arbitrage”).
  • The observer has a real baseline in that domain, not a first look at a crowd.
  • The change is material at that company, and is not yet in estimates and notes.
  • Stories often cluster just before a disclosure — a shelf change, then earnings a few days later — not “buy a hot brand and hold it for a decade.”

何时失败

When it fails

  • 隐藏的分部： Restaurant Brands / Tim Hortons，自述亏损 75 万美元——两个品牌看对了，第三个看不见。
  • 个人偏好： Silicon Image（HDMI），「投资你熟悉的」变成偏见。
  • 数据被污染： 2025 年 Lululemon 与 Trends；交叉验证才能避免套利一个幽灵。
  • 把存量当成动量： 一个 350 亿次播放的标签，本身不说明「正在上升」。
  • 证据与市场路径见 交易库、业绩、街景。事件研究是市场路径，不是他的个人盈亏。
  • The hidden division: Restaurant Brands / Tim Hortons, a self-reported $750,000 loss — two brands right, a third invisible.
  • Personal bias: Silicon Image (HDMI), where “invest in what you know” became a trap.
  • Polluted data: Lululemon and Trends in 2025; without a cross-check you will arbitrage a ghost.
  • Mistaking a stock for a flow: a 35-billion-view hashtag does not, by itself, mean “it is rising.”
  • Evidence and market paths: the trade database, track record, and Street view. The event study is a market path, not his personal P&L.

普通投资者该改写什么

What a normal investor should adapt

可以借用的是观察习惯、基线、交叉验证、物质性检验和定价检验。扫描器和本页的数据工作流，都只是人工研究的提示，不是买入信号。

不要借用的是：2%–30% 的仓位、把储蓄的 25%–30% 用于看跌期权、在没有信息过程的情况下取消止损、以及把审计过的 77% 年化当成可复制的承诺。如果你投资，用一个可以承受损失的独立账户；默认用股票现货而不是期权；仓位小到你无需引用他的数字就能解释；先写论点再行动。模板在 thesis.md。

What you can borrow: the habit of observation, a baseline, corroboration, the materiality test, and the priced-in test. The scanner and the data workflow on this page are prompts for human work, not buy signals.

What you should not borrow: 2–30% position sizes, putting 25–30% of savings into puts, dropping stop-losses without an information process, or treating the audited 77% annual compound as a reproducible promise. If you invest, use a separate account funded with money you can afford to lose; default to cash equity rather than options; size a position so you can explain it without citing his figures; write the thesis before you act. 端到端顺序，以及一条命令

End-to-end order, and one command

  1. 发现。 记下相对基线的真实变化。拉取 Trends（五年）、维基百科，以及相关的 TikTok 标签与亚马逊名次——都带着来源、时间和做法。
  2. 交叉验证。 两个或三个独立平台相对自身基线上升。终身 TikTok 计数和单一 BSR 列入「有结果但无 z」，不充当动量。缺失平台列出，不填空。
  3. 物质性。 打开 10-K 分部。推动不了利润，就停下。
  4. 卖方定价。 跑 Street Consensus Profile。已定价，放下。只有「利基上升且尚未清楚定价」进入下一步。
  5. 论点备忘。 用模板写下论点与退出（信息平价，以及「工具被污染」），再决定是否行动。备忘是提示，不是交易指令。
  1. Discover. Log a real change versus your baseline. Pull Trends (five-year), Wikipedia, and the relevant TikTok tags and Amazon ranks — each with a source, a time, and a method.
  2. Corroborate. Two or three independent platforms rising versus their own baselines. Lifetime TikTok totals and a single BSR sit in “present but no z”; they do not count as momentum. Missing platforms are listed, never imputed.
  3. Materiality. Open the 10-K segment. If it cannot move earnings, stop.
  4. Street / priced-in. Run the Street Consensus Profile. If already priced, stand down. Only “niche rising and not clearly priced” continues.
  5. Thesis memo. Write the thesis and the exit (information parity, and “the tool is polluted”) before any action. The memo is a prompt, not a trade.

--offline uses verified caches only (no new network calls) and is the way to reproduce a memo. The default mode pulls Trends, news, and the Street profile live, and degrades honestly on failure.
