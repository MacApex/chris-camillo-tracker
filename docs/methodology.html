方法论总纲

Methodology synthesis

时差，不是更便宜的估值。

A time gap, not a cheaper multiple.

把本档案已经核实的方法，收成一页总纲：第一性原理、因果链、七条真正要紧的规则、方法何时成立与失败，以及普通投资者该如何改写。证据链到既有页面，这里不重复长文。本页不是投资建议，也与 Camillo、Dumb Money、TickerTags 均无关联。

Everything we have verified, distilled to one page: first principles, the causal chain, the seven rules that actually matter, when the method works and fails, and what a normal investor should change. Evidence lives on the existing pages; this is not a repeat. This is not financial advice, and it is not affiliated with Camillo, Dumb Money, or TickerTags.

因果链

The causal chain

  1. 在真实世界里，观察到相对于你自己的基线（这个社群、这个季节、这个品牌平时的样子）的异常变化——文化、消费、技术或政策。不是相对于一条头条。
  2. 把它连接到一家上市公司：该变化必须大到足以推动这家公司的收入或利润（物质性）。
  3. 用 2–3 个独立平台交叉确认：各平台相对自身历史在上升。一条序列不是论点。终身累计播放量或单一畅销名次不是动量。
  4. 卖方材料只辅助核对已经写下来的论点。沉默或「尚未反映」都不是买入条件。
  5. 卖方行只辅助核对一条已经写下来的论点。它不决定研究是否可以开始。
  6. 信息平价只是一种退出想法。还要写流动性、偿债、估值、跳空、相关敞口和组合亏损上限。来源不可靠是重新评估，不是自动判定生意为假。
  1. Observe a real-world change — culture, consumption, technology, or policy — versus your own baseline for that community, season, and brand. Not versus a headline.
  2. Connect it to a public company where the change is large enough to move revenue or earnings (materiality).
  3. Corroborate on two or three independent platforms: each rising versus its own history. One series is not a thesis. A lifetime view total or a single bestseller rank is not momentum.
  4. Street material only checks a thesis already written. Silence, or “not yet reflected,” is not a condition for buying.
  5. Street rows are an auxiliary check of one written thesis. They do not decide that research may begin.
  6. Information parity is only one exit idea. Also write liquidity, solvency, valuation, gap risk, correlation, and a portfolio-loss limit. An unreliable source means reassessment, not an automatic false business.

任何一关为「否」：停止，或继续观察。展开的决策树、清单与案例见 核心逻辑；十步操作见 方法手册；卖方框架与事件研究见 街景。

A “no” at any gate is a stop, or a decision to keep watching. The full tree, checklists and cases are on Core logic; the ten operational steps are in the Playbook; the Street framework and event study are on Street view.

七条真正要紧的规则

The seven rules that matter

  1. 问的是异常，不是热闹。 没有基线，评论再多也只是噪音。Camillo 对 Crocs 与 UGG 的说法是：他知道「平常」是多少。
  2. 边缘是时差，不是同一个模型上更聪明。 Schwager 把这一章题为 “Neither”：既非传统基本面，也非技术分析。灵感来自林奇，但财务深挖被主动放下，只保留规模与物质性。
  3. 一条序列不是论点，更不是利润。 两个平台各自的 z 是本项目的启发式，不是他公布的规则。终身播放、单一亚马逊名次、以及同一 TikTok 载荷上的第二个客户端，都不算独立需求。2025 年的帖子只是把 Lululemon 产品词当作 Trends 可靠性的例子。
  4. 先核实增量利润，再评估估值。 问这个变化能否变成发行人确认的收入和增量利润；能写出来之后，才评估估值。未知保持待研究。没有「打开分部说明、不要做估值」这条读者规则。
  5. 卖方读数不单独决定放下。 他个人说过，信息一旦「被普遍知道」，他就当作已经在价格里。那是对他做法的转述，不是读者的停止规则。无论所审材料是否反映这条论点，经济、估值和风险研究都继续。明确反映还需要注明日期的原文，以及能对上的盈利假设；整体看多不算。
  6. 不能核实的数字，丢掉。 终身播放量是存量，要动量必须自己做重复取样——本项目不设每日快照例行。宽泛标签（如 #coach、#slime）不可当作品牌计数。查询会被污染（X 去掉 $ 之后，ELF 会严重噪声）。
  7. 不要把他的风险结构当成读者规则。 记者报道 Schwager 审阅过月度账单、Adebambo 同意；那不是对每一笔交易的公开发表审计。他曾说仓位大约 2%–30%、不用止损。那是对他的转述。信息平价只是一种退出想法。分开的账户不能消除家庭风险。本页不设默认仓位，教育上的默认是不用杠杆。
  1. The question is “unusual,” not “busy.” Without a baseline, a lot of talk is just noise. On Crocs and UGGs he has said he knows what ordinary comment volume looks like.
  2. The edge is a time gap, not a smarter take on the same model. Schwager titled the chapter “Neither”: neither traditional fundamentals nor technical analysis. Lynch is the inspiration; the financial deep-dive is dropped; size and materiality remain.
  3. One series is not a thesis, and it is not earnings. Two platforms each with their own z is this project’s heuristic, not a rule he published. A lifetime view total, one Amazon rank, and a second client on the same TikTok payload are not independent demand. The 2025 thread only uses Lululemon product terms as a Trends-reliability example.
  4. Verify incremental earnings, then evaluate valuation. Ask whether the change can become recognized revenue and incremental profit. Valuation comes after that bridge can be written. Unknowns stay needs research. There is no reader rule that says “open the segment note instead of a valuation.”
  5. A Street reading does not by itself mean stand down. He has said that once information is “universally known,” he treats it as in the price. That is a report of his practice, not a reader’s stop rule. Economics, valuation, and risk research continue whether or not the reviewed rows reflect the thesis. Explicit reflection needs a dated quotation and matching earnings assumptions. An overall bullish rating is not that.
  6. Discard unverifiable numbers. Lifetime view totals are a stock; a flow needs repeated snapshots at a cadence you choose — this project does not run a daily routine. Generic tags (#coach, #slime) are not brand counts. Queries get polluted (after X strips the $ character, ELF is noisy).
  7. Do not copy the reported risk structure. Business Insider reported that Schwager reviewed monthly statements and that Prof. Adebambo agreed; that is not a published audit of every trade, and this project did not separate observation, leverage, and concentration. He has said sizing of about 2–30% and that he uses no stops. Those are reports about him, not rules for a reader. 1–3% is not a universal size. A separate account does not remove household risk. No leverage is the educational default. Information parity is only one exit criterion.

何时成立

When it works

  • 变化发生在卖方听得少的地方：青年与女性消费文化、地理口袋（他所说的「地理套利」）。
  • 观察者对该领域有真正的基线，而不是第一次看到热闹。
  • 变化有可能变成那家公司的增量利润。研报里有没有写到，是辅助核对，不是因此停下估值。
  • 往往靠近一次披露：货架变化之后几天就是财报，这是他故事里反复出现的节奏，不是「买热门品牌拿十年」。
  • The change sits where the Street under-listens: youth and female consumer culture, geographic pockets (his “geographical arbitrage”).
  • The observer has a real baseline in that domain, not a first look at a crowd.
  • The change might become incremental profit at that company. Whether notes already mention it is an auxiliary check, not a reason to skip valuation.
  • Stories often cluster just before a disclosure — a shelf change, then earnings a few days later — not “buy a hot brand and hold it for a decade.”

何时失败

When it fails

  • 隐藏的分部： Restaurant Brands / Tim Hortons，自述亏损 75 万美元——两个品牌看对了，第三个看不见。
  • 个人偏好： Silicon Image（HDMI），「投资你熟悉的」变成偏见。
  • 搜索可靠性未经核实： 2025 年的帖子假设自动查询可能影响 Trends，并用 Lululemon 产品词举例。这不是已确立的原因。
  • 把存量当成动量： 一个 350 亿次播放的标签，本身不说明「正在上升」。
  • 证据与市场路径见 交易库、业绩、街景。事件研究是市场路径，不是他的个人盈亏。
  • The hidden division: Restaurant Brands / Tim Hortons, a self-reported $750,000 loss — two brands right, a third invisible.
  • Personal bias: Silicon Image (HDMI), where “invest in what you know” became a trap.
  • Unverified search reliability: a 2025 thread says Trends may be affected by automated queries and uses Lululemon product terms as the example. That is not an established cause.
  • Mistaking a stock for a flow: a 35-billion-view hashtag does not, by itself, mean “it is rising.”
  • Evidence and market paths: the trade database, track record, and Street view. The event study is a market path, not his personal P&L.

普通投资者该改写什么

What a normal investor should adapt

可以借用的是观察习惯、基线、交叉验证、物质性检验和定价检验。扫描器和本页的数据工作流，都只是人工研究的提示，不是买入信号。

不要借用的是：2%–30% 的仓位、把储蓄的 25%–30% 用于看跌期权、在没有信息过程的情况下取消止损、以及把审计过的 77% 年化当成可复制的承诺。如果你投资，用一个可以承受损失的独立账户；默认用股票现货而不是期权；仓位小到你无需引用他的数字就能解释；先写论点再行动。模板在 thesis.md。

What you can borrow: the habit of observation, a baseline, corroboration, the materiality test, and the priced-in test. The scanner and the data workflow on this page are prompts for human work, not buy signals.

What you should not borrow: 2–30% position sizes, putting 25–30% of savings into puts, dropping stop-losses without an information process, or treating the audited 77% annual compound as a reproducible promise. If you invest, use a separate account funded with money you can afford to lose; default to cash equity rather than options; size a position so you can explain it without citing his figures; write the thesis before you act. 端到端顺序，以及一条命令

End-to-end order, and one command

  1. 社会观察。 从这里开始，而不是从卖方筛选开始。短期社交或搜索信号不是利润、现金流或价值。
  2. 证据质量。 分开自述、记者看过的截图和独立文件。周标签不是可获得时间。
  3. 交叉。 只是项目启发式：两个相对自身基线的真实 z。Trends 指数、终身总量、一个名次、同一载荷上的第二个客户端，都不算。
  4. 收入桥。 顾客、件数、净价、确认收入、增量利润、抵消项、税和股数、有日期的预期、估值与下行。未知就留空。没有 1%–2% 的一刀切。
  5. 证伪、期限、风险。 写出预测、截止日期和相反证据。写明期限。信息平价只是一种退出想法。还要写流动性、偿债、估值、跳空、相关性、催化剂推迟；若讨论期权，还要写到期、隐含波动和权利金可能全部损失。价格下跌本身不是加仓理由。教育上的默认是不用杠杆。这些是项目约定，不是已核实的 Camillo 规则。1%–3% 并非普遍合适。分开的账户不能消除家庭风险。
  6. 辅助的卖方核对。 明确反映、部分反映、所审材料未见，或证据不足。沉默不是无知。
  7. 人工决定。 备忘不是买入。
  1. Social observation. Start here, not from a Street screen. A short-term social or search signal is not earnings, cash flow, or value.
  2. Evidence quality. Separate self-report, a journalist-viewed screenshot, and an independent document. A week label is not availability.
  3. Corroboration. Project heuristic only: two real z-scores versus each series’ own baseline. A Trends index, a lifetime total, one rank, and a second client on the same payload do not count.
  4. Revenue bridge. Customers, units, net price, recognized revenue, incremental profit, offsets, tax and share count, dated expectations, valuation and downside. Unknowns stay unknown. No 1–2% cutoff.
  5. Falsify, horizon, risk. Write the prediction, the deadline, and contrary evidence. State the horizon. Information parity is only one exit idea. Also write liquidity, solvency, valuation, gap risk, correlation, catalyst delay, and, if options are even discussed, expiry, implied volatility, and total premium loss. A lower price alone is not a reason to add. No leverage by default. These are project conventions, not verified Camillo rules. 1–3% is not universally suitable. A separate account does not remove household risk.
  6. Auxiliary Street check. Explicitly reflected, partly reflected, not evidenced in the reviewed sources, or insufficient evidence. Silence is not ignorance.
  7. Human decision. The memo is not a buy.

--offline uses verified caches only (no new network calls) and is the way to reproduce a memo. The default mode pulls Trends, news, and the Street profile live, and degrades honestly on failure. The report is a dated Markdown and JSON file with gates, sources, and missingness recorded rather than filled. Sample: 2026-11-13_CROX_Crocs.md. 信任矩阵：什么问题信什么源

Decision matrix: which source for which question

问题该信的源不要当作答案的源
文化讨论是否相对基线在上升？重复取样后的 TikTok 具体标签、干净的 X 近窗、五年 Trends。至少两个。单次终身播放量；宽泛标签；已被新闻占满的话题（往往已滞后）。
搜索兴趣是否在上升？五年 Trends，并用维基百科 z 交叉。2025 年的帖子假设自动查询可能影响 Trends，可靠性未经核实。默认十二个月的 Trends 视图；与其他关键词比较原始 0–100。
货是否在卖？同一 ASIN、同一亚马逊榜单上的重复名次；商品页细分类作为核对。Keepa 价格图；CamelCamelCamel（在此为 403）；把「不在前 50」写成一个估出来的名次。
卖方是否已经在这件事上？street_view 的定价结论，加上新闻 / GDELT 作为「是否已经公开」。把新闻量当成领先的利基信号；在数据不足时当成绿灯。
应用是否在飙升？我们核实的只有「在架」。要名次需日后的付费榜单。把 iTunes 搜索命中当成需求。
这个词是不是太宽？用具体标签与人工判断（#elfcosmetics 而不是 #elf）。把 #coach、#lulu、#slime、去掉 $ 后的 ELF 当成品牌计数。

Optional keys and paid upgrades (not required)
