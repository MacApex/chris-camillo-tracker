仓位、期权、失败，和时代。

Sizing, options, failure, and the times.

仓位

他公开说过按信念在约 2–30% 之间分配，没有止损，论点仍在时可能加仓。2011 年是「大钱」桶与安全账户分开；后来 Stansberry 提到账户在取款后约 600–650 万美元（自我陈述，且与 BI 2020 年 12 月的 970 万美元口径不同，不可强行对齐）。普通投资者不应复制这个区间。

期权结构

期权被当作有限风险的杠杆，常常围绕催化剂（Target × Missoni 隔夜；后来自我陈述的亚马逊财报期权）。新冠是储蓄 25–30% 的指数与赌场看跌。我们没有行权价或希腊值，不会编造。工具箱刻意不自动下单。

时机模型

周期可以从隔夜到数月。退出是分批 10–20%，直到财报和研报反映事实。他用冲浪作比：耐心等大浪。五年 Trends 用来处理季节性；2025 年补上了 AI 污染的警告。

想法从哪来

2011 年：100–200 次观察换 1–2 笔交易，每天夜里数小时。后来：自称每天四小时 TikTok；研究 100% 在 iPhone 上完成（Stansberry）；约一半后期想法来自当时约 3 万成员的 Discord。TickerTags（约 150 万词标签、Twitter 十等分数据流）于 2018 年 6 月 1 日被 M Science / Jefferies 收购；之后他花每年约 1–1.5 万美元买网络流量数据。地理套利（HeyDude）和青年 / 女性视角的盲区，他自己都谈过。

亏损与失效环境

有来源的亏损包括：RBI / Tim Hortons（约 75 万美元，截图被看过）、Silicon Image（流程被偏见压过）、最后悔的那笔（听别人的话砍掉仍成立的看涨期权，代码未公布）。失效环境：卖方已经定价；物质性不够；工具被污染；迷因挤兑（参与过，但不是典型信息差）；危机中的长期消费论点（RBI 拖进 2020 年 3 月）。2008 年他自称「持平或略赚」并做空——自我陈述。

方法的演变，2006 至今

起步是线下观察与《笑傲华尔街》里的消费浪潮（UGG、True Religion、Wii）。2010 年代中期建立 TickerTags，2018 年出售。2020 年新冠把外语一手研究和居家消费主题放到台前。2021 年同时出现截图可查的 Tapestry 仓位，以及自我陈述的 GME / AMC 参与。近年转向 TikTok 与 Discord，并在 2025 年公开承认 Trends 的破裂。

Dumb Money 团队

YouTube / 播客与 Dave Hanson、Jordan McLain 合作。这是内容与社群层，不应自动等同于他的个人交易账户。Discord 是后期想法的重要来源。我们不把节目嘉宾的观点记成他的交易。

对照：林奇、另类数据、学术

彼得·林奇：「走进商场」是共同起点；林奇仍做深基本面，Camillo 明确放弃。另类数据基金（包括收购了 TickerTags 的 M Science）把同样的信息差机构化，速度与覆盖通常快过散户，但也受同样的职业风险约束。学术上：Da, Engelberg & Gao 2011（JF）《In Search of Attention》用 Google Search 作为注意力；Preis, Moat & Stanley 2013（Sci Rep）讨论搜索与市场；Hong, Kubik & Solomon 2000（RAND）与 Hong & Kubik 2003（JF）讨论分析师从众与职业风险；Bhushan 1989 讨论覆盖与规模；McNichols & O’Brien 指出下调慢于上调（中位数 127 天对 98 天）。这些文献支持「卖方会滞后」，但不能证明某一个社会套利规则可交易。

Position sizing

He has said he sizes by conviction in a roughly 2–30% range, uses no stops, and may add if the thesis is intact. In 2011 a “big money” sleeve sat apart from a safer account. On Stansberry the account was “about $6–6.5 million after withdrawals” — self-reported, and not the same figure as BI’s December 2020 $9.7 million; we do not force a reconciliation. A normal investor should not copy this range.

Options structure

Options are described as defined-risk leverage, often around a catalyst (Target × Missoni overnight; a later self-reported Amazon earnings options line). The COVID shorts were 25–30% of savings in index and casino puts. We do not have strikes or Greeks and will not invent them. The toolkit deliberately does not place orders.

Timing model

Holds can run from overnight to months. Exits are described as 10–20% at a time until earnings and notes reflect the fact. He uses a big-wave surfing metaphor: patience. Five-year Trends is how he said he handles seasonality, with a 2025 caveat about AI pollution.

Idea-sourcing workflow

In 2011: 100–200 observations for 1–2 trades a year, hours at night. Later: four hours a night on TikTok, by his account; 100% of research on an iPhone (Stansberry); about half of later ideas from a Discord that then had ~30k members. TickerTags (~1.5 million word tags, Twitter decahose) was acquired by M Science / Jefferies on 1 June 2018; afterwards he has said he spends about $10–15k a year on web-traffic data. Geographic arbitrage (HeyDude) and youth/female blind spots are points he has made himself.

Losses and regimes where it fails

Sourced losses include RBI / Tim Hortons (~$750k, screenshots viewed), Silicon Image (bias over process), and the most-regretful trade (cutting still-valid calls after others’ opinions; ticker unnamed). Failure regimes: the Street has already priced it; materiality is too small; the measuring tool is polluted; meme squeezes (he participated, but they are not typical information-gap trades); a long-dated consumer thesis into a crash (RBI into March 2020). In 2008 he has said he was even or slightly up after going short — self-reported.

Ethos, 2006 to now

It begins with offline observation and the consumer waves in Laughing at Wall Street (UGGs, True Religion, the Wii). TickerTags is built in the mid-2010s and sold in 2018. 2020 puts foreign-language primary research and stay-at-home themes on stage. 2021 holds both a screenshot-visible Tapestry position and self-reported GME / AMC participation. Recent years shift toward TikTok and Discord, with a public 2025 admission that Trends can break.

The Dumb Money team

The YouTube show and podcast are with Dave Hanson and Jordan McLain. That is a content and community layer, not automatically the same as his personal book. Discord is a later idea source. We do not log guest opinions as his trades.

Lynch, alt-data, academia

Peter Lynch: “walk the mall” is the shared starting point; Lynch still does deep fundamentals, Camillo explicitly drops them. Alternative-data firms (including M Science, which bought TickerTags) institutionalise the same information gap — usually faster, and still bound by career-risk incentives. In the literature: Da, Engelberg & Gao 2011 (JF), “In Search of Attention,” uses Google Search as attention; Preis, Moat & Stanley 2013 (Sci Rep) on search and markets; Hong, Kubik & Solomon 2000 (RAND) and Hong & Kubik 2003 (JF) on analyst herding and career risk; Bhushan 1989 on coverage versus size; McNichols & O’Brien on slower downgrades (median 127 days versus 98 for upgrades). These papers support the claim that the Street can lag. They do not validate a particular social-arbitrage rule as a tradable system.
