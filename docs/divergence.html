华尔街为何滞后——以及一套寻找缺口的扫描器

Why Wall Street lags — and a scanner for the gap

卖方并不愚蠢，只是受约束。职业风险、覆盖预算、从业绩指引出发的模型，以及对某些消费者的文化距离，会制造出时间间隔。Camillo 的生涯，就是一场有记录的赌注：这些间隔可以交易。下面的扫描器是手电筒，不是判断。完整文章：research/04-wall-street-lag.md。

The Street is not stupid; it is constrained. Career risk, coverage budgets, models that start from guidance, and a cultural distance from certain consumers create intervals. Camillo’s career is a documented bet that those intervals are tradable. The scanner below is a flashlight, not a judgment. Full essay: research/04-wall-street-lag.md.

结构性的理由

The structural case

  • 职业风险与从众。 Hong, Kubik and Solomon（《RAND Journal of Economics》，2000）：经验较少的分析师会因大胆、错误、偏离共识的预测而受罚，于是他们从众——更靠近共识，也更不及时。Hong and Kubik（《Journal of Finance》，2003）：职业顾虑也会把预测推向乐观。
  • 覆盖是稀缺预算。 它集中在大盘股和已经「有故事」的名字上。一个只占集团 1–2% 的胶水产品线，或一个区域鞋品牌，很容易被覆盖不足。
  • 官方输入是滞后的。 业绩指引、上季收入、排期的渠道核实，确认的是已经发生的事。一支底妆测评或五年 Trends 的突破更早、也更非正式——这正是 TickerTags 存在的理由，也是据他说许多交易台仍不愿据此行动的原因。
  • 搜索并不纯是噪音。 Da, Engelberg and Gao（2011）以及 Preis, Moat and Stanley（2013）给观察 Google 关注度提供了学术分量——同时要记住 Camillo 自己在 2025 年的保留：Trends 现在可能是脏的。
  • 主动管理跑输是背景，不是证明。 SPIVA，经 Insider（2021）引用：到 2020 年的二十年里，刚过 86% 的美国主动股票基金经理跑输标普 1500。这并不能让社会套利成为残差。它确实削弱了「专业人士早就掌握了」这种反对。
  • Career risk and herding. Hong, Kubik and Solomon (RAND Journal of Economics, 2000): less-experienced analysts are punished for bold, wrong, non-consensus forecasts, so they herd — closer to consensus, less timely. Hong and Kubik (Journal of Finance, 2003): career concerns also tilt forecasts optimistic.
  • Coverage is a scarce budget. It clusters on large caps and names that already “have a story.” A glue line that is 1–2% of a conglomerate, or a regional shoe brand, is easy to under-cover.
  • Official inputs are lagging. Guidance, last quarter’s revenue, scheduled channel checks confirm what already happened. A primer review or a five-year Trends break is earlier and unofficial — which is why TickerTags existed, and why, he says, many desks still would not act on it.
  • Search is not pure noise. Da, Engelberg and Gao (2011) and Preis, Moat and Stanley (2013) give academic weight to watching Google attention — with Camillo’s own 2025 caveat that Trends can now be dirty.
  • Active-manager underperformance is context, not proof. SPIVA, as cited by Insider (2021): just over 86% of US active stock-fund managers underperformed the S&P 1500 in the twenty years through 2020. That does not make social arbitrage the residual. It does weaken the objection that “the professionals already have this.”

这个缺口会过期。另类数据部门和懂 TikTok 的分析师已经收窄了其中一部分。正确的结论不是「华尔街永远会迟到」，而是「去看覆盖薄、文化远离中城、信号仍有人类基线的地方」。

The gap is perishable. Alt-data desks and TikTok-literate analysts have narrowed some of it. The right conclusion is not “Wall Street will always be late,” but “look where coverage is thin, culture is far from Midtown, and the signal still has a human baseline.”
