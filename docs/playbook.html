02 · Methodology

A playbook for information-gap investing

Distilled from Camillo’s interviews and from Business Insider’s 2020–2021 features. This is a framework for research, not a mechanical system and not advice. Full note: research/02-methodology-and-playbook.md.

“I solely trade information.”Chris Camillo, Business Insider, December 2020

Schwager titled the chapter on him “Neither,” because the method is neither fundamental nor technical analysis. He looks for a meaningful change — culture, consumption, technology, policy — connects it to a public company, and arbitrages the interval before Wall Street prices it in. Once the fact is “universally known,” he says, it is in the price. The edge is a time gap.

He ignores charts and, except for company size (the needle test), traditional financials. Ideally he would not even look at the stock price on the way in or out. Exits are gradual — “10% or 20% at a time” — as the information reaches earnings, transaction data, and sell-side notes (BI, 2021). Holding periods in his stories range from overnight (Target × Missoni) to many months (Dorel) to on-and-off years (Crocs).

Risk, which popular summaries skip

He teaches a separate “big money” account funded with money the investor can afford to lose. In 2011 he said he used leveraged options on every investment; later talks still treat options as defined-risk leverage (you can lose the entire premium). He has said he uses no stop-losses, because he trades information, not price — and may add if the price falls and the thesis is intact. Position sizing, in 2020: generally 2% to 30% of the portfolio, by conviction. Adebambo’s review of the audited book found almost 5× index volatility. Kirkus, in 2011, already argued that call options, not mall-walking, were the under-advertised engine.

Known breaks, in his words: Restaurant Brands / Tim Hortons (the hidden division); Silicon Image (personal bias); Lululemon / Google Trends in 2025 (corrupted alternative data). One series is not a thesis.

Ten steps you can actually run

Steps 6–8 are where people get hurt if they skip the risk bucket and the needle test. This toolkit does not place trades.

Build watchfulness, not a ticker list

Inhabit a domain you already know. The work is a baseline: what “normal” conversation looks like, versus a breakout. He has said he knows, from years of watching Crocs and Uggs, the ordinary volume of comments each season.

Log observations, not tips

When something looks different, write what changed, where you saw it, and which public companies sit on either side. In 2011 he described 100–200 observations a year collapsing to one or two trades.

Run the needle test before the romance

Is the change large enough for that company? This is the one place a 10-K is worth opening: segment size, not a DCF. A slime craze can matter to Elmer’s and still be a rounding error, or not, at Newell.

Ask whether the Street already knows

Transcripts, notes, CNBC, a five-year Google Trends window (he warned the default 12-month view is seasonal). If the fact is in the model, the imbalance is gone. If Trends disagrees with card data or clerks, believe the cross-check.

Do physical or conversational channel checks

Clerks, users, regional pockets (“geographical arbitrage”). His e.l.f. work was not only a viral video; it was Target aisles. A community can stand in for an expert network if you ask precise questions.

If you invest, separate the risk

A dedicated account, money you can afford to lose. This is his explicit teaching and the only responsible reading of a method that uses options and concentration.

Match expression to a catalyst, not a mood

Many of his stories cluster just before a disclosure. Options around earnings are a professional technique with well-known failure modes. Observational work does not require them. Kirkus’s objection was that the book made this look easier than it is.

Exit on information parity, not a price target

Scale out as the fact lands. Do not use a chart “break” as the rule. Do leave if the reason was wrong, incomplete, or based on dirty data. Adding to a loser is only coherent when the information, not the ego, is unchanged.

Keep a known-failure file

Bias toward your hobbies, hidden segments, stale alt-data, and boredom trades. The method’s integrity is the willingness to change your mind.

Do not automate the judgment

The divergence scanner can show when search heat diverges from consensus. That is a prompt — the beginning of step 4 — not a buy signal. His work product is a narrative with a catalyst, not a score.
