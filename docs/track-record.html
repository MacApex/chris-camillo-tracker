什么已核实，什么是自我陈述，什么我们未能证实

What is verified, what is self-reported, and what we could not confirm

从 2 万美元到 8,000 万美元的头条总额，混杂了经独立审阅的对账单、后来自我陈述的数字，以及不同的账户定义。诚实的读法是把这些层次分开。完整笔记在仓库 research/01-track-record.md。

Headline totals from $20,000 to $80 million mix independently reviewed statements, self-reported later figures, and different account definitions. The honest reading keeps those layers apart. The full note is in the repository at research/01-track-record.md.

经审计的锚点。 Jack Schwager 审阅了自 2006 年 8 月起的月度券商对账单（起始余额 84,000 美元）。2006–2020 年平均年化复合回报：77%，同期标普 500 为 7.6%。到 2020 年 5 月超过 2,100 万美元（含已提取部分）；到 2021 年 5 月累计利润 4,200 万美元，这是 Schwager 为《商业内幕》做的更新。Insider 还看过 2014–2019 年的 1099 表，显示约 560 万美元收益。Biljana Adebambo 教授审阅了同一批对账单，表示同意，并指出波动几乎是标普 500 ETF 的五倍，期权很可能是成分之一。（Laila Maidan，《Business Insider》，2021 年 8 月 18 日。）
The audited anchor. Jack Schwager reviewed monthly brokerage statements beginning in August 2006 (starting balance $84,000). Average annual compounded return, 2006–2020: 77%, versus 7.6% for the S&P 500. More than $21 million by May 2020, including withdrawals; cumulative profits $42 million by May 2021, after a Schwager update for Business Insider. Insider also viewed Form 1099s for 2014–2019 showing about $5.6 million of gains. Prof. Biljana Adebambo reviewed the same statements, agreed, and noted almost five times the volatility of an S&P 500 ETF, with options a likely ingredient. (Laila Maidan, Business Insider, 18 August 2021.)

如何看待后来的数字

How to hold the later numbers

宣传和访谈里的总额——3,000 万美元（CMC，2022）、7,000 万美元（Chart Academy）、8,000 万美元（2026 年谈话）、一个 1,000 万美元的亚马逊期权故事、一个 3,000 万美元的新冠篮子——也许是诚实的。它们与 Schwager 的信不是同一回事。他从 2011 年起就说得很清楚：钱被提取过，高风险子账户坐落在一个更大的账户里，而且有过增资。把「2 万美元靠读 TikTok 变成 8,000 万」当成一条单一的复利路径，是误导。

Promotional and interview totals — $30 million (CMC, 2022), $70 million (Chart Academy), $80 million (2026 talks), a $10 million Amazon options story, a $30 million COVID basket — may be honest. They are not the same as the Schwager letter. He has been explicit, including in 2011, that money was withdrawn, that a high-risk sleeve sat inside a larger account, and that capital was added. Treating “$20k to $80 million by reading TikTok” as a single compounded path is misleading.
